# Monthly Subscription Plan

One plan. One price. The client sees only the monthly number — no tiers, no
feature comparison table, no "basic vs pro" confusion.

---

## The plan

| | India | International |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | **₹1,999 / month** | **$29 / month** |
| Files included | 10 per month | 10 per month |
| Rows per file | up to 50,000 | up to 50,000 |
| Turnaround | within 24 hours | within 24 hours |
| Extra file | ₹150 each | $2 each |

### What is included

- Clean, remove duplicates, fix emails, fix phone numbers
- The full report workbook every time (Report + Cleaned + Rejected)
- WhatsApp or email delivery
- Unlimited small corrections on the same file
- Cancel anytime, no lock-in

### What is not included

- Email verification against real mailboxes (that needs a paid API)
- Data entry or filling missing information
- Working inside Google Sheets
- Files above 50,000 rows (quoted separately)

## Founding client offer

For the first 5 clients only:

> **₹999 / month, locked forever.**

Say it like this: *"Normally ₹1,999. You are one of my first five clients, so
₹999 for as long as you stay."*

This is the single best way to get your first paying customers, and the price
lock costs you nothing later because you will raise the normal price.

---

## How the client pays

**India:** Razorpay payment link or UPI Autopay mandate.
Create one link, send it, they pay by UPI / card / netbanking. Money arrives in
your bank in 2 working days. Do not build your own payment system.

**International:** Stripe payment link or PayPal subscription.

**Simple start (no gateway needed):** send a UPI request on the 1st of every
month and keep a note of who paid. This is fine for the first 5 clients. Move to
autopay once you have 10, because chasing payments wastes hours.

---

## The rules that protect you

Put these in writing before the first payment, even in a simple WhatsApp message.

1. **Payment first, work after.** The month starts on the payment date.
2. **10 files per month.** Extra files are charged at ₹150 each. No exceptions —
   one client sending 40 files will eat your whole month.
3. **One revision per file.** If they send wrong data, that is a new file.
4. **No refund for the current month.** They can cancel and stop next month.
5. **48-hour notice on big files.** Files above 20,000 rows may take longer.
6. **They own their data, you do not keep it.** Delete the file after delivery —
   say this openly, it builds trust and protects you.

Rule 2 and rule 6 are the important ones.

## Your monthly routine

Do this on the 1st of every month. It takes 15 minutes total.

1. Send the payment link to every active client.
2. Mark who has paid in a simple sheet: name, amount, date, files used.
3. When a file arrives: clean it, check the Report sheet, send it back.
4. Note the file count against their 10.
5. On the 25th, tell anyone near the limit: "you have 2 files left this month."
6. Delete the client files from your computer after delivery.

A simple sheet is enough. Do not build software for this until you have 20+
clients — a spreadsheet is faster and cannot break.

## When to raise the price

- After 5 clients: stop the founding offer, charge ₹1,999.
- After 10 clients: try ₹2,999 on new clients only. Keep old clients at their
  current price — that is the reward for trusting you early.
- When you are turning work away: raise the price, do not work more hours.

## Money maths (why this works)

At ₹1,999 and about 15 minutes of real work per file:

| Clients | Monthly income | Your time |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | ₹9,995 | ~6 hours |
| 10 | ₹19,990 | ~12 hours |
| 20 | ₹39,980 | ~25 hours |

Twenty clients is a serious side income for roughly three hours a week. That is
the whole reason to sell monthly instead of per file — the same client pays you
again without you finding a new client.
